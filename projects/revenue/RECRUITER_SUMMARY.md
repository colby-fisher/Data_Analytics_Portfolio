30-second recruiter summary — Revenue Dashboard

Problem: Provide ops/finance a concise dashboard to monitor revenue trends, product performance, and regions that need attention.

Approach: ETL + SQL aggregations for reliable KPIs, interactive Streamlit dashboard with filters and anomaly markers, and clear business-facing notes.

Tools: Python (pandas), SQLite, Plotly, Streamlit

Business value: Faster identification of revenue dips and top products to prioritize operational or merchandising actions.
