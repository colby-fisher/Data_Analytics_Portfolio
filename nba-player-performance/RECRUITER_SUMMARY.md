30-second recruiter summary

Problem: Compare shot-selection and scoring value for three NBA rookies to identify role differences and prioritized development opportunities.

Approach: Standardize shot-location data, compute zone-level KPIs (attempts, FG%, shot share, points per attempt), and compare profiles with clear visuals and evidence-backed recommendations.

Key result: Kon Knueppel is perimeter-oriented with the highest three-point rate; Dylan Harper shows the strongest conversion in the restricted area; Cooper Flagg has elevated mid-range volume with lower efficiency—mid-range selection is a priority for film review.

Tools: Python (pandas), Jupyter, Matplotlib/Seaborn, Plotly, Streamlit (mini-app)

Business value: Quick, defensible inputs for scouting/development prioritization and a reproducible workflow for future players.
